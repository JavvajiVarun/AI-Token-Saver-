AI Token Saver is installed

Keep long AI chats light: summarize and continue in a fresh chat with one click.

1. Pin it to your toolbar

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of the address bar.
  2. Find AI Token Saver and click the pin next to it.
Checking…

2. How it works

  • Open a chat on Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. Every new message re-sends the whole chat, so long chats use more of your limit.
  • When a chat passes about 20,000 tokens, a card appears. Click Summarize & continue.
  • The extension asks that chat for a summary, opens a new chat, and sends the summary there. Your old chat stays in your history.
  • Click the coin icon any time to see how many tokens you've saved, check the current chat's size, or hand off early. Right-click it → Options to change the threshold, auto-send or the summary prompt.

3. Your data stays on your computer

  • To estimate a chat's size, the extension reads the text of the chat page you have open on one of these four sites.
  • When you click Summarize & continue, it types the summary request into that chat, sends it, and pastes the reply into a new chat on the same site. It never sends anything anywhere else.
  • Your settings and the summary being moved are stored only in your browser. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
